Sempere Choir Management System is a private system for members of the Sempere Choir.

We use Google Sign-In only to verify your email address and match it to your choir membership record. We do not access your Google contacts, files, or any other Google data. We store only the information needed to run the choir: member records, attendance, messages, and contributions. This data is not sold or shared with third parties.
